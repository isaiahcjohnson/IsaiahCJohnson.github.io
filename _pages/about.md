# Welcome! 

I am a Postdoctoral Associate at Rice University and a member of the [*Executive Policy Research Group*](https://executivepolicy.org). I received my Ph.D. in Politics from Princeton University in 2026. I study American politics with a focus on representation, elite behavior, and the institutions that shape both.

My research asks how identity and uncertainty shape the strategies of political elites, with particular attention to Multiracial politicians. My job market paper, [*Jack of All Trades, Master of Some: Racial Competency and the Limits of Multiracial Representation*](https://www.isaiahcjohnson.com/files/2026-09-24-Multiracial-Formall.pdf) (under review), develops a formal model of descriptive representation as an agency problem, showing how uncertainty about racial competence shapes incumbents' strategies. At Rice, I extend this agenda to the executive branch, examining elite behavior with respect to unilateral policymaking. My work has appeared in *Presidential Studies Quarterly* and is conditionally accepted at *Political Behavior*, with additional research under review at the *Journal of Politics*.

I am committed to fostering inclusive and analytically rigorous classrooms and to mentoring students in American politics, race and ethnic politics, and formal and quantitative methods. In 2025, I received the *George Kateb Preceptor Award* for excellence in teaching at Princeton.

I received my B.S. and M.A. in Political Science from the University of Houston in 2019 and 2021.
